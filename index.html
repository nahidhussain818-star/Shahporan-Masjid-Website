About Us

A community rooted in faith & service.

Shahporan Masjid & Islamic Centre has been serving Muslims in East London for decades. Our doors are open for the five daily prayers, Jumu'ah, Tarawih during Ramadan, and a wide range of educational and community activities for all ages.

We are a registered charity (Reg. No. 1057627) operating as the Shahporan Masjid Trust, sustained entirely by the generosity of the community we serve.

Knowledge

Weekly Circles

Almost every evening in the main prayer hall there is an informal circle which can be heard in both the male and female prayer areas. During British Summer Time, the circle is typically held after 'Asr or Maghrib. For the rest of the year, it follows the 'Isha prayer.

Topics include Qur'anic tafsir, prophetic biography, fiqh, and spiritual reminders. Everyone is welcome — no registration needed.

About Islam

Islam is a way of life centred on the worship of One God (Allah) and following the guidance of His final Messenger, the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ.

The Five Pillars

  • Shahādah — Declaration of faith.
  • Salah — Five daily prayers.
  • Zakāh — Annual charity for the needy.
  • Sawm — Fasting in Ramadan.
  • Hajj — Pilgrimage to Makkah, once in a lifetime if able.

Articles of Faith

  • Belief in Allah, the One God.
  • Belief in His angels.
  • Belief in His revealed books.
  • Belief in all the prophets and messengers.
  • Belief in the Day of Judgement.
  • Belief in divine decree (Qadar).
